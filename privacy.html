隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

最近更新:2026 年 8 月 · Last updated: August 2026

1. 我们收集的信息

Sudogku(下称"本游戏")由 Belistory Games 开发运营。我们不注册账号、不收集姓名、邮箱、手机号等个人身份信息。为了改进游戏体验,本游戏通过 GameAnalytics 收集以下匿名统计数据:

  • 设备信息:设备型号、操作系统版本(不含设备唯一标识符)
  • 使用数据:游戏会话、关卡进度事件(开始/完成/失败)
  • 大致地理位置:由 GameAnalytics 服务器根据 IP 解析出国家/地区级位置(IP 不被存储)

We do not require an account and do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, email or phone number. We use GameAnalytics to collect anonymous gameplay statistics: device model and OS version, gameplay events (session, level progress), and country-level location derived from your IP address (the IP itself is not stored).

2. 我们如何使用信息

  • 统计活跃用户与留存,评估游戏质量
  • 分析关卡难度,改进关卡设计
  • 排查崩溃与功能异常

The data is used solely to measure engagement and retention, balance level difficulty, and diagnose issues. It is never used for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

3. 信息共享与第三方服务

除 GameAnalytics 作为数据处理方外,我们不与任何第三方共享你的信息。GameAnalytics 的隐私实践见其官方政策(gameanalytics.com/privacy)。

We do not share your information with third parties other than GameAnalytics, which acts as our data processor. See gameanalytics.com/privacy for their practices.

4. 本地数据

游戏进度、设置等数据仅保存在你的设备本地,卸载游戏即删除,我们不会上传这些数据。

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the game deletes them; we never upload them.

5. 你的权利

根据你所在地区的适用法律(如欧盟 GDPR),你对与我们处理的数据享有以下权利:

  • 访问与导出:要求查看我们持有的与你相关的统计数据
  • 删除:要求删除相关数据——由于数据完全匿名且不与身份关联,我们通常只能指引你通过卸载游戏、重置设备广告标识符等方式实现
  • 撤回同意:卸载游戏即可终止一切数据收集
  • 投诉:你有权向当地数据保护监管机构投诉

行使上述权利请联系 zhitingintl@gmail.com,我们会在 30 天内响应。

You have the right to access, export, and request deletion of your data, to withdraw consent (by uninstalling the game), and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. As all collected data is anonymous and not linked to your identity, deletion requests can typically only be fulfilled by resetting your device's advertising identifier or uninstalling. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

6. 数据保留

匿名统计数据在 GameAnalytics 服务器上的保留期不超过 12 个月;本地数据在你卸载游戏时删除。我们不会在自己的服务器上存储任何用户数据。

Anonymous analytics data is retained by GameAnalytics for no more than 12 months. Local data is deleted upon uninstall. We store no user data on our own servers.

7. 国际传输

统计数据由 GameAnalytics 的服务器处理,可能传输至你所在国家/地区以外。GameAnalytics 按 GDPR 要求提供相应的数据保护保障。

Analytics data may be processed on GameAnalytics servers outside your country, with safeguards as required by GDPR.

8. 数据安全

所有统计数据通过 HTTPS 加密传输;我们遵循最小化收集原则,只收集改进游戏所必需的信息。

All analytics data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We follow data minimization principles and collect only what is needed to improve the game.

9. 儿童隐私

本游戏面向全年龄,不有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的任何个人信息。如你发现孩子向我们提供了个人信息,请联系我们删除。

The game is suitable for all ages and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. 政策变更

我们可能不定期修订本政策。重要变更会通过游戏内公告或本页面醒目提示告知,并在上方注明更新日期。继续使用本游戏即视为接受修订后的政策。

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in-game or prominently on this page, with the revision date noted above.

11. 联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问,请联系我们:zhitingintl@gmail.com
